Step 1 — Split the answer

The answer becomes several individual sentences. Each sentence is treated as a rough claim.

Step 2 — Compare the claim with evidence

Supported claim ✓

WHO recommends ACE inhibitors as initial treatment.

WHO recommends ACE inhibitors initial treatment
Unsupported claim ✗

Patients should take 5mg of amlodipine twice daily and monitor potassium weekly.

5mg amlodipine twice daily potassium weekly

How the check works

claim_words = set(...)

evidence_words = set(...)

overlap =
    len(claim_words & evidence_words)
    / len(claim_words)

supported =
    overlap >= 0.35
Talking: “This is a second safety net. I split the answer into claims and check whether important words from each claim appear in the retrieved evidence.”

Important: This is a simple lexical heuristic. It is not a full medical fact checker.

DECISION GUIDE

What If the Test Is Bad?

Bad Result What it means What to check
Low Precision@3 Relevant evidence is not being retrieved. 1. PDF parsing
2. Chunking
3. Embeddings
4. Top-k
5. Hybrid retrieval
Good retrieval + bad safety Retriever works, but refusal decisions are weak. Revisit confidence-score separation and tune the threshold.
Good retrieval + safety, bad claim detection Evidence is good, but generated answers may drift. Improve grounding and claim verification.
Everything looks good The system is behaving as expected. Record the actual numbers for Day 5.
